## Cohorts, snapshots and residual semantics

The [Round 3](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/round3.html),
[Round 4](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/round4.html), and
[Round 5](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/round5.html) guidelines verify:

| Round | Total topics | Newly introduced IDs | CORD-19 release | All four roles uniquely compatible? |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 40 | 36–40 | May 19, 2020 | No |
| 4 | 45 | 41–45 | June 19, 2020 | No |
| 5 | 50 | 46–50 | July 16, 2020 | No |

These are new information needs on changing snapshots of the same collection,
not a new collection and not merely new labels on topics 1–30. The proposed
ranges are therefore verified, but no source/query coverage assertion follows.

All rounds after the first use residual evaluation: previously judged
topic-document pairs are removed before scoring. The
[Round 3 archive](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/archive/archive-round3.html)
explicitly identifies its stored files as the scored versions after removal,
including articles whose IDs changed. The
[Round 3](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/qrels3.html),
[Round 4](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/qrels4.html), and
[Round 5](https://ir.nist.gov/trec-covid/qrels5.html) judgment explanations
confirm that changed-ID articles were removed before scoring and that many
lists consequently contain fewer than 1,000 documents. Later collections are
not strict supersets: works can disappear or receive new `cord_uid` identifiers.
The qrel's document-round component specifies its target snapshot's IDs.

The metadata distinguishes automatic query construction from feedback using
official prior judgments. An automatic label is not evidence that a pipeline
was untouched by prior development or that its methods match Round 1. Conversely,
new-topic status does not automatically make an entire feedback submission a
documented reproduction of the original pipeline.
