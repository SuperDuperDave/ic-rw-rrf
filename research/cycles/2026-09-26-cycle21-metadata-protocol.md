## Question, frame and distinguishing prediction

Can the complete official Round1 submission frame identify at least two other
teams with multiple automatic outputs, giving an outcome-independent input set
for a fixed grouped-output comparison? Successful metadata identification adds
a feasible distinct finite question; missing, ambiguous or excessive inputs
close this route without an effectiveness conclusion. Two groups is a scope
requirement to avoid another selected pair, not a power or genericity threshold.

The proposed later primary would compare each team's fixed equally weighted
group+S fusion with the arithmetic mean of all its member+S hybrid P@10 values,
then equally average across eligible teams and the same30Round1 queries. S is
the existing BioinfoUA-emb source. Canonical ties, depth100, one-based k60 and
binary P@10 stay fixed. No member is selected by quality. Shared pair-label
coefficients must be aggregated before sharp completion bounds; family intervals
cannot generally be averaged sharply. Prediction: a positive aggregate lower
bound. Nonpositive upper excludes positive aggregate benefit; otherwise unresolved.
This is a proposed finite development contrast, not the previous F−S contrast,
fresh-query transfer, error-independence identification or a population claim.

## Outcome-independent eligibility

1. Census every official Round1 team and submission in the complete archive and
   run metadata. Use exact official team association, not run-name clustering.
2. Count a run as automatic only if official metadata explicitly says so.
   Manual/feedback/unspecified types remain recorded but are not eligible.
   Record method descriptions to distinguish team membership from claims of
   shared architecture; no method-similarity threshold selects runs.
3. Exclude from primary the team owning BITEM_BL (already inspected) and the
   team owning BioinfoUA-emb (the fixed S partner). Resolve ownership uniquely
   from metadata; ambiguity stops the gate. Do not acquire new excluded runs.
4. An eligible team has at least two explicitly automatic runs; include ALL its
   automatic members. No best baseline, renamed role matching, convenience team
   selection, score-table lookup or dropping a run after its outcomes are seen.
5. Require unique run IDs, metadata/archive agreement for every eligible run,
   complete team census, and no ambiguous duplicate submission identity. The
   number of eligible groups/runs and methods are results of this inventory.
