## Headline

**v6.0 strictly improves on v5.0 across every tested ensemble configuration
and on both TREC DL collections.** It matches Vanilla RRF within bootstrap
noise on most sweeps and significantly beats Vanilla in one sweep (TREC DL
2019 n=4, p=0.028).

The cross-ensemble mean (TREC DL 2019):

| Variant | NDCG@10 mean | Δ vs v5.0 | Δ vs Vanilla |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vanilla RRF | 0.3910 | +0.0193 | — |
| IC-RRF v5.0 | 0.3717 | — | -0.0193 |
| **IC-RRF v6.0 (REF)** | **0.3919** | **+0.0202 (+5.4%)** | +0.0009 |

The v5.0 MRR weakness is healed. The "IC-RRF beats RRF" claim is now
empirically defensible across ensembles rather than fragile to setup.

## Falsified Hypotheses During the v5.0 → v6.0 Transition

Three explicit hypotheses were tested and falsified before v6.0 was
settled. Documented in detail in the spec; summarized here for the audit
trail.

1. **Multi-signal regime detection (logistic alpha × coverage entropy) strictly dominates single-signal.**
   *Falsified.* Best v2 config (mu=0.45, tau=0.04, h_weight=0.5) reached
   cross-mean 0.4030 vs v1's 0.4013 — within noise. One sweep produced a
   significant *loss* against Vanilla (2019 n=7, p=0.013). Net: single-signal
   rho is sufficient at this evaluation scale.

2. **A single ensemble-level statistic (rho) is sufficient to predict which fusion wins.**
   *Falsified.* TREC DL 2019 n=4 and TREC DL 2020 n=4 have indistinguishable
   rho (0.491, 0.495), but Vanilla loses on the first and wins on the second.
   At least one collection-level property influences outcome that is not
   observable from rank statistics alone.

3. **Sharpening the alpha curve (lo=0.45 instead of 0.35) strictly dominates the baseline.**
   *Falsified.* lo=0.45 dropped 2019 n=4 to 0.3737 (lost v5's home-basin
   advantage). Cross-mean 0.3912 vs baseline 0.3919. The narrower
   "homogeneous regime" excluded the productive rho=0.49 region.

These falsifications are the structural reason v6.0's algorithm has the
specific shape it does: single-signal regime, asymmetric piecewise-linear
alpha curve with lo=0.35 / hi=0.60.
