# Cycle21 — a feasible input frame and a two-sign counterexample

The metadata gate identifies **29 eligible teams and all 81 of their automatic
submissions**, without downloading any new ranking. Independently, an exact
synthetic construction shows that the previous control invariants permit both
positive and negative natural-minus-control effects. Neither result establishes
an effective new retrieval method. Buddy's challenge to the scientific value of
the proposed census has been assessed and returned to the same Opus session.

## Complete input frame, before outcomes

The [protocol](../../_sessions/cycles/2026-09-26-cycle21-metadata-protocol.md)
was [hash-frozen](../../_sessions/evidence/2026-09-26-cycle21-metadata-freeze.json)
before opening the new metadata. The two complete official indexes contain
143 submissions from 56 teams: 100 automatic and 43 manual. Excluding the already
inspected BITEM team and the fixed S partner's BioinformaticsUA team leaves
29 teams with at least two automatic submissions. All their automatic members
are included: 23 triples and six pairs. Thirteen other teams have only one
automatic output and twelve have none. Three eligible teams also have a manual
sibling, which remains excluded.

Two independent captures and parsers agree on every run ID, team, type, publisher
MD5 and archive association. The [inventory](inventory.json) records the full
frame and exclusions. The [independent review](../../_sessions/cycles/2026-09-26-cycle21-metadata-review.md)
summarizes all eligible teams' methods and actual input constraints, with direct
links to the official sources. Publisher MD5s have not yet been checked against
the unopened ranking bodies.

The count is within the proposed 180-run budget. Individual body sizes are not
published in the two pages, so byte feasibility is still unknown. Any subsequent
acquisition needs enforced streaming limits and all-member/query coverage checks;
no favorable subsampling or silent member removal is allowed.

Team identity is an administrative grouping, not a certificate of related
architectures or independent errors. Methods vary query fields, document views,
candidate subsets, neural models and feedback. Some members already fuse other
rankings; one team's system consumes another team's scores. Some descriptions
do not identify what differs between member runs. Officially automatic output
also does not imply label-free model development.

No new run, qrel, topic, document-inventory, corpus or performance-report body was
opened. Method prose incidentally mentions an external STSbenchmark result and
general quality claims; these were not selection criteria. A later pooling-source
search exposed organizer-level comparative prose and a system-rank correlation
in snippets, but no performance table was opened and no eligibility changed. Existing quartet
outcomes and the reuse of the same 30 topics were already known.

The two raw captures have different hashes only because of runtime scripts.
Removing those scripts makes the entire paired pages identical. A normalization
repair preserved literal topic-field names that generic HTML stripping had
removed; no factual census field or eligibility changed. The
[crosscheck](../../_sessions/evidence/2026-09-26-cycle21-metadata-crosscheck.json)
records this distinction. Raw HTML/method narratives remain ignored; public
receipts retain hashes and safe metadata.

## The control design does not force the sign

The [synthetic note](../../_sessions/cycles/2026-09-26-cycle21-pairing-note.md)
constructs full depth-100 A/D/T/S lists at k60, with a common candidate set and
fixed grade/membership profiles. Nine head documents are unchanged. Two equally
relevant documents can swap within D and T; a third, nonrelevant document has a
fixed score. The four legal states select a relevant tenth document three times
and the nonrelevant document once, always with a strict score margin.

Against that exhaustive four-state mean, choosing the first state as natural
gives **+1/40 P@10**, while choosing the fourth gives **−3/40**. The same unknown
head labels cancel under every completion. A separate coordinator integer
reconstruction verifies all score numerators and head identities in the
[check receipt](../../_sessions/evidence/2026-09-26-cycle21-pairing-check.json).

This is a constructive possibility result. It refutes an implication from the
preservation constraints to one sign; it does not predict how often either sign
occurs. The four-state expectation is not asserted to equal cycle20's particular
256 seeded draws. The earlier measured negative remains a distinct finite result.

The correct fixed term in F=(a+d+t)/3+s is a/3+s, not a+s. Independent D/T
shuffles can change their summed contribution multiset, and the top-ten cutoff
can move. Thus Opus's proposed score-concentration explanation remains a
hypothesis; neither the empirical sign nor this synthetic witness identifies it
as the cause. No new tuning of the closed quartet follows.

## Proposed next decision

The candidate empirical question compares each team's group+S fusion, at fixed
total group weight, with the mean P@10 of every member's simple hybrid with S.
It would use new outputs on the same reused topics. It selects neither a best
member nor a best team. A finite mean over this complete frame is a different
estimand from BITEM F−S, source independence, or fresh-query transfer.

Buddy recommended rejecting the census, arguing that it lacks a decision
consumer and will be unresolved because many runs were not pooled. The first
challenge sharpened the research decision. The second is unsupported: contrast
uncertainty depends on signed document membership after cancellation, not a
run's pool-contributor flag. A second exact fixture has two uncertain team
contrasts whose aggregate is identically zero; independent integer reconstruction
confirms it. A proposed correlation on the old controls would not identify a
cause either. The [design review](../../_sessions/cycles/2026-09-26-cycle21-group-design-review.md)
and [integration](../../_sessions/cycles/2026-09-26-cycle21-integration.md)
preserve the disagreement and checked corrections.

The assessed Buddy critique was read by the same Opus5.5/high session at Relay135,
consumed139. The coordinator selects one hybrid census, retaining all eligible
members and the same primary comparator. A positive finite result can justify
examining the documented Round2 S route for a new-topic test of this exact rule;
a nonpositive or unresolved result closes investment in this specified route.
Standalone S remains a contextual practical reference. No extra S-free arm,
parameter sweep or pool-based selection is added.

Actual acquisition requires the separate cycle22 code/protocol freeze. The
prospective input contract treats100as a retention cap and includes valid shorter
submissions; official guidelines allow1–1000documents per query. No actual bodies
were read before this decision. Metadata and synthetic tasks are complete; the
continuous research goal moves to a bounded empirical execution.
