## What the gate protected

The [protocol](../../_sessions/cycles/2026-09-25-cycle19-inventory-protocol.md)
was [hash-frozen](../../_sessions/evidence/2026-09-25-cycle19-inventory-freeze.json)
before later metadata was opened. It required uniquely documented original-team
automatic roles, all eligible new topics, and no substitution using effectiveness.
No team score report or individual relevance judgment was inspected. Incidental
qualitative organizer commentary is disclosed in the inventory notes and did
not change a decision. All twenty proposed new topic IDs were verified, but no
source coverage assertion was made without data.

The same run name did not imply the same retrieval pipeline. Conversely, the
coordinator initially mistook BioInfo's title/abstract restriction and embeddings
for later changes; the existing Round1 documentation showed they were already
present. That claim was corrected. Round2's S role is compatible at the stated
metadata level; the absent BITEM roles independently fail the quartet.

Twelve worker-captured and four coordinator-captured complete official HTML
pages have byte/hash receipts. One additional browser-page capture hit its
1.5MB cap; the partial body is explicitly not absence evidence. The complete
official archive supplies the census, while web-rendered method sections supply
the descriptions. No source crawl or enlarged download was needed to rescue
this route. Raw runtime/HTML remains ignored.

Two bounded scouts propose one different question: preserve every source's
candidate set and grade-at-rank profile, then permute judged variant IDs within
exact-grade strata to change rank alignment. The adopted Opus refinement also
conditions on the fixed source-membership mask, preserving reciprocal mass
within each class. Unknown IDs stay fixed. Natural
F would be compared with a fixed finite mean of controls, with H/S references.
This would use labels to construct a mechanism diagnostic on reused queries;
it would not replace transfer or establish a deployable improvement.

remains sharp. A broader warning about incompatible label completions did not
