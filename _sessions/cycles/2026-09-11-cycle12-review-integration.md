## Interpretation adopted

Separate three decisions:

1. **Case validity:** a defined question, initial evidence boundary, traceable
   additional record and an independently checkable answer or abstention rule.
2. **Comparison value:** policies with the same access/tools/budget could make
   consequential different acquisitions. Include the strongest simple route.
   Different record choices alone do not establish better answers or lower cost.
3. **Observed benefit or failure:** an outcome of a frozen evaluation, not a
   condition for selecting its cases. Do not search until a baseline fails.
