## Corrections to the review

- **Output agreement does not identify computation or selection.** The memo
  calls direct computation demonstrated. It is compatible with the observations;
  necessary certificate use and the internal sequence remain unidentified.
  Likewise, its token-count argument cannot establish that the model did not
  weigh support. Observed thinking counters are resource metadata, not a
  mechanistic diagnostic. Endpoint comparison can avoid checking intermediate
  transitions; it still reads the endpoint field.
- **A recomputable task is not a universal impossibility result.** The memo's
  claim that no evidence-selection signal is measurable in such tasks is too
  broad. This experiment measures returned decisions and consistency. It does
  not isolate internal evidence use or causal copying effects. Whether evidence
  saves computation, changes errors under a bound, or supplies a better checking
  route would require another design; none was measured here or scheduled now.
  This is an all-correct baseline checkpoint, not proof of a null effect.
- **Do not invent recomputability labels for the old retrieval runs.** Those
  experiments evaluate ranked document IDs against pooled relevance judgments;
  they do not supply the proposed answer-producing judge, an exact answer
  semantics, or a computation budget. An unspecified binary flag and “handful”
  gate cannot turn them into a validated evidence-selection dataset. Their
  observed specialist association was already limited by judgment coverage and
  candidate-access coupling. Even a post hoc stratum difference would not prove
  a masked causal minority-evidence effect. No such relabeling or effect search
  was performed.

These corrections preserve the useful stopping recommendation without accepting
its stronger mechanism and cross-task claims. The review did not change a score,
repair an output or create a successor fusion method.
