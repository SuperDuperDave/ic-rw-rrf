## Hypothesis and comparison

Changing only N from 4 to 64 may create distinct sequential state trajectories
in each of four matched templates. The competing possibility is that cycles,
identical final states or a demonstrated shortcut defeat the intended contrast.
The comparator is N=4 in the same template, parameters and final predicate.
Primary observations are exact state traces, final states, Boolean truths and
the explicitly specified local gate. There are no model responses, accuracy
estimates or statistical independence claims in this local experiment.

A constant Boolean over the observed prefixes0..64, or equal Boolean answers
at N=4 and N=64, is descriptive evidence. Neither alone adds a parking criterion
to the committed design. A demonstrated source/algebraic shortcut is a separate
reason to park. This clarification was agreed with the independent checker
before either implementation enumerated the actual panel. Absence of a found
shortcut does not establish computational hardness.
