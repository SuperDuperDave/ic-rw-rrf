## Proposed distinct question

Return to retrieval evaluation: which missing relevance judgment would most
reduce uncertainty about a *fixed* fusion comparison? Disagreement may locate
valuable questions without implying the minority is correct. This is an applied
resource question about real ranked lists; it does not relabel qrels as factual
answers or require finding a model failure. We have not computed this new pair's
bound or acquisition order yet.

Candidate finite design: A = canonical k60 RRF of bm25, bm25_tuned, tfidf,
ql_dirichlet; B = those four plus the already-acquired SPLADE++ list. Preserve
their retained depths (lexical30/SPLADE1000), canonical one-based fusion and
stable document-ID ties. The unequal depth is part of this fixed applied pair,
not a claim of an isolated source-quality or minority effect. All common queries
per year are included, without selecting on labels or difficulty.

Use the explicit top10 RBP contribution with p=4/5, linear grade gain g/3 for
grades0..3, and zero weight below10. This is a diagnostic metric, not full RBP or
the old nDCG. Exact rational weights make interval certificates auditable.
For each query, combine shared document identity first: a_d=w_B(d)-w_A(d).
Known contributions give C; unknown gains may independently take0,1/3,2/3,1.
The exact range is [C+sum(min(a_d,0)), C+sum(max(a_d,0))] over unknown documents.
Its width is sum|a_d|. An exact new label removes |a_d| of width regardless of
the value. With equal label costs and fixed budget, largest |a_d| is the strong
simple width-minimizing baseline; same-position shared documents cancel.

Compare its projected residual width at budgets0,1,3 with pooled-head judging
(minimum of the two ranks, ties by document ID), both restricted to the same
unjudged top10 union. Include zero-influence documents in the pool and report
them. Uniform selection has an analytic expected width, without seed sweeps.
Measure current exact finite-panel bounds from existing qrels; report per-year
and per-query results. No new grades are acquired or invented. Width reduction
is algebra, not an observed relevance improvement, sign-resolution guarantee or
LLM/agent benefit. A ranking winner is certified only if its lower bound is>0
or upper bound<0. Single-system judging can implement all these policies.
