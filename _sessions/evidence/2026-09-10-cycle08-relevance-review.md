## Follow-up: is difficulty calibration necessary?

On a separate challenge, the reviewer clarified that a difficulty-only checkpoint
is necessary only for a question about naturally occurring solver errors. It is
not a prerequisite for every concrete evidence-aggregation test.

A candidate alternative asks whether a coordinator can extract warranted support
from execution certificates and preserve that distinction when an erroneous
certificate is repeated. Local checkers establish validity; the coordinator sees
transitions with validity labels and corruption locations hidden. Copy identity
records provenance, never correctness. This differs from cycles04/05's supplied
reliability numbers: the coordinator must inspect evidence content.

The appropriate comparator is one verifier receiving the same program and
certificates under the same total inference budget. Exact machine verification
is a separate ceiling and usually the practical solution for executable tasks.
Verification, resistance to duplicated evidence, and superiority of multiagent
collaboration remain distinct questions. This suggestion has no frozen grammar,
data, experiment or implied performance claim yet.
